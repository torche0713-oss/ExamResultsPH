How to Verify Your PRC Rating

Step-by-step guide to checking your official board exam rating on the PRC LERIS portal.

ℹ️ Official verification is done through the PRC LERIS portal at prc.gov.ph/leris. This guide walks you through the process.

What You Need

  • PRC LERIS account (register if you don't have one)
  • Notice of Admission (NOA) or exam reference number
  • Valid email address and password
  • Internet connection and a web browser

Step-by-Step Guide

1

Go to the PRC LERIS Portal

Open your web browser and go to https://www.prc.gov.ph/leris. This is the official online service portal of the Professional Regulation Commission.

2

Log In to Your Account

Enter your registered email address and password. If you don't have an account yet, click "Register" and follow the registration process. You will need a valid email and personal information.

3

Navigate to Verification of Rating

Once logged in, look for the "Verification of Rating" tab or link in the main dashboard. It is usually located under the "Transactions" or "Services" section.

4

Enter Your Examination Details

Select the exam you took from the dropdown menu. Enter your Notice of Admission (NOA) number or your reference number if required. Select the exam date and month.

5

View and Print Your Rating

Your official rating will be displayed on screen. You can print this page or save it as a PDF for your records. The verified rating includes your board exam rating and passed status.

Tip: If you encounter any issues, try clearing your browser cache or using a different browser (Chrome or Firefox recommended). For technical support, contact PRC directly through their official channels.

Alternative Method

You can also verify your rating through the PRC official website's examination result page. However, the LERIS portal provides the most secure and up-to-date verification.

Warning: PRC does not charge for verification of rating. Be wary of third-party websites asking for payment to verify your rating.
