About ExamResults.PH

Your trusted source for Philippine board exam results.

Our Mission

ExamResults.PH was created to make Philippine board exam results more accessible, searchable, and useful for examinees, schools, researchers, and the general public. We aggregate and organize results from official sources so you can find what you need quickly.

What We Do

We collect, organize, and publish exam results from official sources including:

  • Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) — All licensure board exams
  • Civil Service Commission (CSC) — Career Service Examination (CSE-PPT)
  • National Police Commission (NAPOLCOM) — Entrance and promotional exams
  • Supreme Court of the Philippines — Bar examination results
  • Other examining bodies — SPLE and specialty exams

Our Sources

All data published on ExamResults.PH is sourced from official government websites. Every exam article includes a direct link to the official source. We update our data as soon as official results are released.

Trust Signal: Every exam page displays a "✓ Official Source Verified" badge and includes a direct link to the PRC or agency official result page, along with the date and time of last update.

Editorial Policy

ExamResults.PH is committed to accuracy and transparency. Our editorial principles:

  • All data is sourced from official government channels
  • Source links are provided on every exam page
  • We display last-updated timestamps for transparency
  • Corrections are published prominently when errors are identified
  • We do not modify or alter official result data
  • We clearly distinguish between official data and editorial content (guides, FAQs, etc.)

Disclaimer

ExamResults.PH is an independent aggregator and is NOT affiliated with the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), Civil Service Commission (CSC), National Police Commission (NAPOLCOM), the Supreme Court of the Philippines, or any other government agency.

While we strive to ensure all information is accurate and up to date, we recommend always verifying critical information with the official government source. For official results, please visit the respective agency's website.

Privacy Policy

ExamResults.PH respects your privacy. We do not collect personal information from visitors. We do not use tracking cookies beyond standard analytics. Any data you submit (search queries) is used only to provide results and is not stored or shared with third parties.
