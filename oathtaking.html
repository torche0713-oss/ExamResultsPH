Oath-taking & Registration

Everything you need to know after passing your board exam — from oath-taking to getting your PRC license.

🎉 Congratulations, new passer! Here is your complete guide to what happens next.

1. Wait for the Oath-taking Schedule

Oath-taking ceremonies are typically scheduled 2–4 weeks after the release of results. The schedule is announced by PRC on their official website and through regional offices. Check your exam's result page for the specific date.

Upcoming Oath-taking: LET March 2026 — April 25-26, 2026. Other exams vary. Check individual exam pages for specific dates.

2. Prepare Your Requirements

Bring the following documents to the oath-taking ceremony:

  • Notice of Admission (NOA) — printed copy
  • Government-issued valid ID (passport, driver's license, UMID, etc.)
  • 2x2 ID picture with white background (at least 2 copies)
  • Passing certificate or result slip (if issued)
  • Ballpen for forms

3. Attend the Oath-taking Ceremony

The ceremony is usually held at PRC regional offices or designated venues. Arrive early. The program includes:

  • Opening remarks and messages from PRC officials
  • Mass oath-taking led by a PRC representative
  • Distribution of certificates (if applicable)
  • Reminders on registration and licensing
Important: Oath-taking is mandatory. If you miss your scheduled date, you may need to attend a special oath-taking ceremony or wait for the next scheduled date.

4. Register for Your PRC License

After oath-taking, you need to file your initial registration to get your PRC professional license. Follow these steps:

1

Go to PRC LERIS

Log in to your LERIS account at prc.gov.ph/leris.

2

File Initial Registration

Select "Initial Registration" from the services menu. Fill out the online form with your personal details and exam information.

3

Pay the Registration Fee

The initial registration fee is ₱600 for most professions. Payment can be made online via GCash, Maya, or through partner banks (payment centers).

4

Set an Appointment

After payment, set an appointment schedule at your chosen PRC regional office for document verification and ID capture.

5

Claim Your PRC License

On your appointment date, go to the PRC office. Have your documents verified, your picture taken, and your license will be processed. Claiming usually takes 1-2 weeks.

5. What's Next?

Once you have your PRC license, you can:

  • Apply for professional jobs in your field
  • Renew your license every 3 years (CPD units required)
  • Join professional organizations
  • Apply for further specializations or board certifications
