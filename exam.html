Oath-taking & Registration

Next steps for new passers

Schedule: Oath-taking will be held on April 25-26, 2026 at venues across all regions.
Requirements: Bring your Notice of Admission, valid ID, and 2x2 ID picture.

Registration for the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) license will open immediately after oath-taking. The initial registration fee is ₱600 for new professionals. Online appointment via LERIS is required.
